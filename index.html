Welcome to PulseTones. This experience uses the Presage SmartSpectra SDK to measure physiological signals (pulse rate, respiration, and HRV) via remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) directly from your video feed.

🔒 Privacy-Preserving Architecture:
  • All video frames are analyzed strictly on-device in-memory.
  • No video, facial images, or raw camera recordings are ever stored, written to disk, or transmitted to any external server.
  • No biometric identification or facial recognition is performed.
⚠️ Non-Medical Disclaimer: PulseTones is strictly an artistic, bio-acoustic generative soundscape for wellness, reflection, and aesthetic delight. It is NOT a medical device, provides no diagnosis or treatment, and must never be used for clinical decision-making.
